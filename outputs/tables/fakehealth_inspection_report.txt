9 DEFENSIBILITY
A Domain: broadly similar health information, but FakeHealth mixes general medical stories/releases while CoAID is COVID-specific and includes news/claims.
B Period: strongly different for dated items (2009-2019 vs CoAID 2020); undated FakeHealth content is uncertain.
C Labels: defensible for within-FakeHealth author-rule contrasts, not equivalent ground truth across datasets.
D Engagement: observed tweet/reply counts align structurally, but query strategy, API/search era, platform ecology, observation window, and missingness differ.
E Confounds: topic/content type; label/reviewer process; era/platform changes; search/window censoring; content/date missingness; sample/engagement scale; CoAID release duplication; story vs release sources.
[INFERENCE] Suitable as a qualified non-COVID comparator for restricted content-level within-dataset contrasts in observed tweet/reply engagement—not for causal period claims or primary retweet/user/network/cascade comparisons.
