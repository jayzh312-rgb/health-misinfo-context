# Protocol: FakeNewsNet engagement under the frozen estimand (extension_v1, round 2)

Written 2026-10-12 before any model was fitted to FakeNewsNet tweet counts. Mirrors `src/run_primary_engagement_models.py`, `src/run_version_aware_engagement_analysis.py` and Methods 2.4–2.5 of the frozen manuscript. Frozen baseline untouched; outputs only under `outputs/extension_v1/`; progress log under `logs/`.

## Declaration on prior observation

Feasibility medians were already observed on 2026-10-12 (round 1, Task 3a): median tweet-id counts of 77.5 (PolitiFact Fake), 7.5 (PolitiFact Real), 12 (GossipCop Fake), 45 (GossipCop Real), and zero-tweet shares of 9.3%, 34.6%, 3.5%, 6.3%. Because the direction of the raw contrast is therefore already known, no rule below is left discretionary: population, outcome, design, family-selection rule, standardization, dependence graph, resampling, seeds, interval method, contrast direction and sensitivity are all fixed here and the script implements them without options.

## Rules

1. **Item.** One row per `id` per subset from `data/raw/fakenewsnet/{politifact,gossipcop}_{fake,real}.csv`. The two PolitiFact ids present in both the fake and real files (`politifact14940`, `politifact14920`) are excluded as label-conflicting. PolitiFact and GossipCop are analysed separately and never pooled.
2. **Outcome.** `unique_post_count` = number of distinct tweet IDs in the row's `tweet_ids` field (tab-separated; duplicates within a row counted once). Rows with an empty field have count 0 and are retained. Retweets and replies are not available in the local copy, so there is no reply outcome, no retweet count and no any-post-or-reply indicator; the hurdle occurrence component refers to posts only.
3. **Label.** Fake = 1 from the file name; the FakeNewsNet label constructs (PolitiFact fact-checker verdicts; GossipCop site scores) are inherited and not verified locally. Labels enter only the design matrix and class tabulations after the dependence graph is built.
4. **Design.** Intercept and Fake indicator only. FakeNewsNet has no release structure, so no release fixed effect exists; standardized means therefore coincide with fitted class means, and the estimand is still computed by the row-level-product rule for comparability.
5. **Family selection.** Candidates Poisson (diagnostic), NB2 and hurdle (logit + zero-truncated NB2) are fitted with the frozen `fit_one`/`fit_hurdle` optimisers (same bounds, multistart, convergence, Hessian and boundary checks). The frozen `select` rule applies: hurdle if it is stable and improves full-outcome AIC over NB2 by more than 2, or if NB2 is unstable; otherwise NB2. The family is fixed per subset before any bootstrap draw.
6. **Estimand.** R = mean_i m(1, X_i) / mean_i m(0, X_i) over all rows including zeros, with m = P(Y>0|a,X)·E(Y|Y>0,a,X) for the hurdle, row-level products averaged before the ratio (`point_metrics`). If a hurdle is selected, the standardized occurrence probabilities and conditional positive means by class are also reported with intervals from the same draws.
7. **Dependence.** Overlap graph with rows as nodes and an edge whenever two rows share at least one tweet ID; connected components (including singletons) are the resampling units. The component summary records component count, size distribution, class composition and the largest component's share of post memberships.
8. **Uncertainty.** Unstratified pairs bootstrap of components: each draw samples K components with replacement (all rows of a selected component, repeats repeated), refits the fixed family by warm start from the full-sample parameters with the frozen same-draw multistart retry (`refit_checked`), re-standardizes on the draw's own rows, and is accepted only if stable and finite. Seeds: `SeedSequence([42, ordinal, attempt])` with ordinal 0 = PolitiFact, 1 = GossipCop. Target 1,000 successful refits; attempts capped at 1,250; failures recorded and excluded. Intervals: 2.5th and 97.5th linear empirical quantiles.
9. **Contrast.** For each subset s and each reference r in {CoAID A_primary_strict, FakeHealth HealthStory}: C = R_s / R_r, with the interval from the full Cartesian product of the subset's 1,000 accepted draws and the reference's 1,000 saved accepted draws in `outputs/tables/version_aware_bootstrap_refits.csv`, computed with the frozen `independent_product` (log differences, linear quantiles, equal weights), exactly as the frozen CoAID/FakeHealth contrast. Reference point estimates are the saved 0.2528 and 0.8403. C < 1 means a larger relative Fake deficit in the FakeNewsNet subset than in the reference. The two references share no draws with each other or with FakeNewsNet.
10. **Duplicate-title sensitivity.** Rows whose whitespace-normalized title appears more than once within the subset are all dropped; the fixed family is refitted on the remainder and the point ratio reported next to the primary point ratio (no bootstrap). This is a diagnostic for repeated or placeholder pages, not a replacement population.

## Known collection differences, stated as limitations

FakeNewsNet collected tweets by searching Twitter for each article's title, so an item's count depends on title searchability and on the crawl date; CoAID and FakeHealth used different linkage procedures. FakeNewsNet items span roughly 2016–2018 for GossipCop and a wider range for PolitiFact, versus 2020 for CoAID and 2009–2018 for FakeHealth. No replies or retweets are available, so the dependence graph uses shared tweet IDs only and secondary outcomes cannot be mirrored. Tweet-ID lists may be truncated by the original collection. Labels are different constructs from both reference datasets. These ratios are observational dataset-context quantities; they do not identify a causal period or platform effect, and no result here alters a frozen estimate.
