# Protocol: extension_v1 round 4 — reviewer checks

Written 2026-10-13 before any label- or outcome-dependent computation for this round. All outputs under `outputs/extension_v1/` (`tables/`, `reports/`, `protocols/`, `scripts/`, `figures/`). Nothing frozen changes; no hyperparameter reselection; every refit uses the locked configuration from `outputs/models/*/selection_lock.json` and the saved split files under `outputs/splits/`. Descriptive and diagnostic only; nothing here replaces a frozen estimate.

## Check 1 — Findability (CoAID A, label-blind construction, class tabulation)
Population: the 5,337 `A_primary_strict` entities (`data/processed/coaid_news_primary_version_aware.csv`). Publisher-resolution status is assigned label-blind by the frozen identity helper `build_family_map` in `src/audit_publisher_heldout_feasibility.py` applied to the prepared metadata of `coaid_text_modeling_ready.csv` (publisher source, article host, article URL), collapsed to resolved (identified family or identified account proxy), unresolved (platform publisher) and missing (no source metadata). First-recorded release is the earliest member release (`add_release_columns`). Originating fact-check site for Fake entities is the lower-cased host of `fact_check_url` (`www.` stripped), where recorded. Outputs: share of entities with at least one unique observed post by class × status, by class × first release, and (Fake only) by fact-check site; title analyzer-token-length summaries (N, mean, median, p25, p75, p90) by class × status using the scikit-learn default word analyzer on the stored `title`. Counts and shares only.

## Check 2 — Length as a shared cue
Targets and frozen models: within-context holdouts (CoAID 702, FakeHealth 330) with `final_*_natural.joblib` (LR) and `within_*.joblib` (SVM); cross-context all-eligible (CoAID→FakeHealth 1,647; FakeHealth→CoAID 3,577) with `frozen_*_natural.joblib` (LR) and `transfer_*.joblib` (SVM); COVID-Rumor clean (3,667) using the saved predictions in `outputs/predictions/covid_rumor_external_transfer_predictions.csv` with labels joined from the NEWS table exactly as the frozen script did; FakeNewsNet title-only clean (PolitiFact 1,025, GossipCop 22,114) using the saved predictions in `outputs/extension_v1/tables/fakenewsnet_external_transfer_predictions.csv` (LR only; no title SVM exists). Scores for the first two target groups are recomputed by transform-only prediction with the frozen pipelines (fitting patched out). Length = scikit-learn default analyzer token count of the exact predictor text each model saw. Bins: within-target quintiles of token count (ties by rank). Per bin: N, predicted-Fake rate at the frozen threshold, actual Fake rate, macro F1, ROC-AUC (blank where one class is absent). Spearman correlation between log(1 + tokens) and the model score per model and target. Length-only baseline: a **new diagnostic model**, scikit-learn LogisticRegression on the single feature log(1 + tokens), fitted on each source training pool (combined-text pool for combined-text targets; title-only pool for the FakeNewsNet targets), class_weight balanced, C = 1, seed 42, and scored on the same targets; its ROC-AUC is reported next to the frozen models and must not be read as a competing classifier.

## Check 3 — Bootstrap shape and the dominant cluster
Saved draws only: `version_aware_bootstrap_refits.csv` (A_primary_strict, 1,000 successful) and `fakenewsnet_engagement_bootstrap_refits.csv` (PolitiFact, GossipCop, 1,000 each). `largest_component_draws` is the number of times the dominant component entered the draw. Report: distribution of inclusions (0, 1, 2+), the ratio's quantiles (2.5, 25, 50, 75, 97.5) conditional on each inclusion class, 20-bin histogram counts of log ratio, and the number of local maxima of a Gaussian-kernel density (Scott bandwidth) as a bimodality indicator. One figure per context (`figures/round4_bootstrap_shape_<context>.png/.svg`): histogram of the ratio coloured by inclusion class. Title audit, label-blind: PolitiFact titles normalized by whitespace; component = the 84-row dominant component of round 2; report exact-duplicate share, near-duplicate share under the frozen rule (bigram Jaccard ≥ 0.95, ≥ 8 tokens) and a relaxed rule (≥ 4 tokens), mean pairwise bigram Jaccard within the component and within the rest, and the ten most frequent normalized titles in the component with counts (titles are public news headlines; no post IDs).

## Check 4 — Mean versus typical item
Zero-inclusive unique-post counts by class for CoAID A, FakeHealth, PolitiFact and GossipCop (round-2 loader, label-conflict ids excluded): share zero, median, 75th and 90th percentiles, mean, and P(random Fake count > random Real count) with ties counted one half (Mann–Whitney probability). Descriptive only.

## Check 5 — Truncation and the FakeHealth signal
Step a (transformer environment, label-blind): with the frozen tokenizer (`load_tokenizer` in `src/audit_transformer_feasibility.py`, pinned revision, offline cache) and the frozen pair-packing allocation (`title_body_allocation`: 512 budget, 4 special tokens, full title kept, body prefix fills the remainder), compute for every FakeHealth row the number of body tokens kept and the character offset in `body_text` where the kept prefix ends, using fast-tokenizer offsets. Step b (classical environment): build `text_before_cut` = title + blank line + body[:offset] and `text_after_cut` = body[offset:], refit the locked FakeHealth LR configuration (cfg_21) on the saved training pool for each representation, score the saved holdout, and report macro F1, ROC-AUC and AP next to the frozen 0.6752 / 0.7701 / 0.5707. Term audit: for the 50 largest-|coefficient| terms of the frozen FakeHealth LR, the share of their training-pool occurrences (vectorizer analyzer n-grams) that fall after the cut.

## Check 6 — Lexical signal reliability
For each dataset, refit the locked natural LR configuration on each of the five saved CV training folds (`cv_fold >= 0 and != f`), take the coefficient vector over the shared unigram vocabulary of `text_shared_unigram_coefficients.csv` (4,174 terms; terms absent from a fold's vocabulary are excluded pairwise and the coverage reported), and report the ten pairwise Spearman correlations with mean, minimum and maximum, next to the cross-dataset Spearman of −0.003.

## Check 7 — Rounding consistency
Every displayed number in `review_package_v3/manuscript.md` text, Table 1, Table 2 and the figure labels of Figures 1–4 is matched to its saved source value (v2 provenance table, key-results table, extension tables, figure source CSVs) and the half-up rounding at the displayed precision is compared with the displayed string. Mismatches are listed with the proposed edit for the next rebuild; the package is not edited in this round.

Limitations fixed in advance: Checks 1 and 4 are descriptive; the length-only baseline is a diagnostic, not a candidate model; fold refits (Check 6) and truncation refits (Check 5) reuse locked settings and saved partitions and are not new selections; conditional bootstrap distributions (Check 3) condition on the original resampling multiplicity and are not alternative intervals.
